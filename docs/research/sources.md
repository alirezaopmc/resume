# Sources

Accessed 18 October 2026. A claim in the research notes cites one of these. Primary means the party that made the measurement or owns the specification. Secondary means a retelling, a vendor test, or a career-office guide.

## Primary

- Ladders, *Keeping an Eye on Recruiter Behavior* (2012 eye-tracking study). PDF: <https://www.bu.edu/com/files/2018/10/TheLadders-EyeTracking-StudyC2.pdf>
- Ladders, *Eye-Tracking Study* (2018). PDF: <https://www.theladders.com/static/images/basicSite/pdfs/TheLadders-EyeTracking-StudyC2.pdf>
- Ladders, press release of the 2018 study, 6 November 2018. <https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ladders-updates-popular-recruiter-eye-tracking-study-with-new-key-insights-on-how-job-seekers-can-improve-their-resumes-300744217.html>
- Nielsen Norman Group, Jakob Nielsen, “F-Shaped Pattern For Reading Web Content (original eyetracking research)”, 16 April 2006. 232 users. <https://www.nngroup.com/articles/f-shaped-pattern-reading-web-content-discovered/>
- Nielsen Norman Group, “F-Shaped Pattern of Reading: Misunderstood, But Still Relevant” (later clarification: the F is one pattern, and headings can change it). <https://www.nngroup.com/articles/f-shaped-pattern-reading-web-content/>
- Typst, PDF reference (tagged PDF by default; `--pdf-standard ua-1`). <https://typst.app/docs/reference/pdf/>
- Typst 0.14.0 changelog, 24 October 2025 (tagged PDF default). <https://typst.app/docs/changelog/0.14.0/>
- JSON Resume schema (`basics`, `work`, `education`, `skills`, `projects`, `awards`, `certificates`; ISO-8601 dates in the data model). <https://github.com/jsonresume/resume-schema/blob/master/schema.json>
- open-ATS v0.2.0 source: scoring table and section keywords in `ats_score/checks_ats.py`, column detection in `ats_score/extract.py`, JSON shape in `ats_score/report.py`. Release 25 June 2026. <https://github.com/NoahMustafa/open-ATS/releases/tag/v0.2.0>

## Secondary

- MIT CAPD, “Resume checklist and worksheet” (10–12 pt, 0.5–1.0 in margins, one page, two pages with an advanced degree or 10+ years). <https://capd.mit.edu/resources/resume-checklist/>
- MIT CAPD, “Make your resume ATS-friendly” (no graphics, tables, or text boxes; font at least 10 pt; test by reading the plain text). <https://capd.mit.edu/resources/make-your-resume-ats-friendly/>
- Sourabh Bajaj, `sb2nov/resume` README and `sourabh_bajaj_resume.tex` (one-column software resume; Education, Experience, Projects). <https://github.com/sb2nov/resume>
- RenderCV, “ATS Compatibility” (their own 20-PDF test on Typst output; Affinda, Extracta, Klippa). <https://docs.rendercv.com/ats_compatibility/>
- open-ATS README (same penalties as the source, written for users). <https://github.com/NoahMustafa/open-ATS/blob/v0.2.0/README.md>

Hosted checkers (Jobscan, Teal, Resume Worded) are named by MIT CAPD as upload-and-compare services. This repo does not call them.

- Indiana State Career Center, résumé writing guide (drop “I,” “my,” and “me”; start with a verb). <https://indianastate.edu/sites/default/files/2024-12/career-center-resume-writing-guide.pdf>
- Wentworth Institute of Technology, Resume Grammar Guide (lean sentences; no first-person pronouns in bullets). <https://coopsandcareers.wit.edu/resources/10476/>
- MIT CAPD toolkit PDF (one or two pages; do not use first-person statements that start with “I”). <https://cdn.uconnectlabs.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/123/2025/01/MIT-CAPDs-Career-Toolkit-Crafting-an-effective-resume.pdf>
- University of Pittsburgh Career Central, 12 February 2025 (475–600 words called a sweet spot, “not policy,” about one page). <https://careercentral.pitt.edu/blog/2025/02/12/resume-and-cv-writing-tips-applicant-tracking-systems-ats/>
- Cultivated Culture, résumé statistics (retells the TalentWorks word-count sample; career level was not reported). <https://cultivatedculture.com/resume-statistics/>
- Jobscan, “Are You Guilty of Resume Keyword Stuffing?” (keywords come from a posting; a skill list is not a checklist). <https://www.jobscan.co/blog/resume-keyword-stuffing/>
